AFFIDAVIT OF ORGANIZATIONAL INTENT AND FINANCIAL COMPLIANCE

State of North Carolina

I, Stephan Ward, being duly sworn, hereby affirm the following regarding the nonprofit corporation National AI Advancement for Human Innovation, organized under the North Carolina Nonprofit Corporation Act (Chapter 55A):

1. Charitable and Scientific Purpose

The corporation is organized and shall be operated exclusively for charitable, educational, and scientific purposes consistent with Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Its mission is to advance the ethical and public-benefit development of artificial intelligence through research, education, and dissemination of non-proprietary knowledge and tools for the benefit of the public.

No part of the organization’s activities shall be conducted for the private benefit of any individual. All programs shall be structured to serve a broad public interest in compliance with federal and North Carolina law.

The formation of this organization predates any subsequent federal executive actions relating to artificial intelligence policy and is grounded solely in charitable and scientific intent under existing nonprofit law.

2. Private Inurement and Compensation Clarification

No part of the net earnings of the corporation shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to, any private individual, except that the corporation may pay reasonable compensation for bona fide services actually rendered.

The affiant acknowledges that:

  • Compensation may be paid for legitimate services provided to the corporation
  • Such compensation must be reasonable, documented, and approved by the Board of Directors
  • Compensation shall not constitute profit distribution or private gain

Any personal income earned by the affiant in his separate capacity as an independent contract software engineer shall be independent from the nonprofit corporation and shall not constitute a distribution of nonprofit earnings.

The corporation shall remain fully compliant with Section 501(c)(3) requirements and the North Carolina Nonprofit Corporation Act.
